⌚ Import from Samsung Health
📲 Samsung Health doesn't allow direct app connections, but you can export your data as a ZIP file and upload it here — the app will read your steps, calories, heart rate, sleep and weight automatically!
📋 How to Export from Samsung Health:
1
Open Samsung Health app on your phone
2
Tap your Profile picture (top right)
3
Tap ⚙️ Settings → scroll down to "Download personal data"
4
Select data types: Steps, Heart Rate, Sleep, Body composition, Exercise
5
Tap "Request" — Samsung will email you a ZIP file (usually within minutes)
6
Download the ZIP → extract it → upload the CSV files below
